A tarot card illustration.
Style: soft watercolor with gentle pastel tones. Warm, dreamy, introspective atmosphere. Muted gold accents. The overall mood is contemplative and healing, never dark or frightening.

Composition: vertical tarot card format with a thin elegant gold border. The card number "XV" is subtly placed at the top. No text or card title on the image.

Mood: Recognizing one's own patterns of attachment, the chains we choose to keep, shadow work. The image should feel like a quiet moment of self-reflection, as if the viewer is looking into a mirror of their inner world.
