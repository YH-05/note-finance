# 女帝 (The Empress)

## Prompt A: 全体図（冒頭ヒーロー用）

A tarot card illustration.
Style: soft watercolor with gentle pastel tones. Warm, dreamy, introspective atmosphere. Muted gold accents. The overall mood is contemplative and healing, never dark or frightening.
Color palette: lavender, soft rose, cream, muted gold, dusty blue.
Composition: vertical tarot card format with a thin elegant gold border. The card number "III" is subtly placed at the top. No text or card title on the image.

Subject: A graceful woman seated in a lush garden filled with wheat, flowers, and flowing water. She wears a crown of stars and holds a heart-shaped shield. The garden is abundant and alive with soft light filtering through trees.

Mood: Nurturing abundance, self-compassion, the beauty of caring for oneself and others. The image should feel like a quiet moment of self-reflection, as if the viewer is looking into a mirror of their inner world.
