# 戦車 (The Chariot)

## Prompt A: 全体図（冒頭ヒーロー用）

A tarot card illustration.
Style: soft watercolor with gentle pastel tones. Warm, dreamy, introspective atmosphere. Muted gold accents. The overall mood is contemplative and healing, never dark or frightening.
Color palette: lavender, soft rose, cream, muted gold, dusty blue.
Composition: vertical tarot card format with a thin elegant gold border. The card number "VII" is subtly placed at the top. No text or card title on the image.

Subject: A determined figure riding a chariot pulled by two sphinxes (one light, one dark). A canopy of stars above. The city walls are behind them. The figure wears a crescent moon breastplate and holds no reins — they guide by will alone.

Mood: Moving forward despite inner conflict, the courage of holding opposites together. The image should feel like a quiet moment of self-reflection, as if the viewer is looking into a mirror of their inner world.
