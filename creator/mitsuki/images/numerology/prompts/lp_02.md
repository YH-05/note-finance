# ライフパス2 — 調和/協力

## Prompt A: 全体図（冒頭ヒーロー用）

A mystical landscape illustration representing numerological energy.
Style: soft watercolor with gentle pastel tones. Warm, dreamy, introspective atmosphere. Muted gold accents. The overall mood is contemplative and healing. No human figures. No numbers or text in the image.
Color palette: lavender, soft rose, cream, muted gold, dusty blue.
Composition: vertical format (2:3 ratio), an ethereal landscape with depth and gentle luminosity. Soft vignette at edges.

Subject: A tranquil lake at twilight reflecting a crescent moon perfectly — the moon above and its mirror image below creating a symmetrical composition. Two graceful willows lean toward each other from opposite banks, their branches almost touching over the water. Fireflies drift gently between them. The water is absolutely still, creating perfect balance.

Mood: Gentle receptivity, the beauty of perfect balance, the quiet strength of harmony and partnership. The image should feel like a quiet portal into an inner world — a dreamscape that resonates with the viewer's soul.

## Prompt B: クローズアップ（解説内用）

A close-up detail from a mystical watercolor landscape.
Style: soft watercolor with gentle pastel tones. Warm, dreamy, introspective atmosphere. Muted gold accents.
Color palette: lavender, soft rose, cream, muted gold, dusty blue.
Composition: square format (1:1 ratio), a cropped intimate view focusing on the crescent moon reflected in the perfectly still water, with fireflies hovering above the surface. Soft bokeh background. No text.

Subject: A crescent moon mirrored in glass-still water, tiny fireflies creating dots of warm light around the reflection, willow leaves barely touching the surface.

Mood: Gentle receptivity, the beauty of perfect balance, the quiet strength of harmony and partnership. Quiet intimacy, as if leaning closer to examine a meaningful detail in a dream.
