## 1. Sector Overview

### Market Size & Growth
- US telecom services market: **$451.7B in 2025**, growing at **5.88% CAGR** to **$601.2B by 2030** (Mordor Intelligence)
- Key growth drivers: 5G rollout, AI data center connectivity demand, BEAD program ($42.45B), FWA expansion

### 5G Deployment Status
- **300M+ Americans** (90%) covered by 5G low-band from all three tier-1 carriers
- **210-300M** covered by 5G mid-band (C-band)
- mmWave: major metro areas only (outdoor, limited range)
- Over half of global 5G traffic expected on mid-band by 2026

### Broadband Market Shift: Fiber vs. Cable
- Cable broadband: ~75M residential subscribers (2024), losing share
- Fiber passings: record **99.7M homes** by end of 2025 (~60% of households)
- Cable net losses: -2.03M (2024), -1.05M (2025), -734K (2026 projected)
- FWA subscribers: **~13.5M total**, growing 18% in H1 2025
- Starlink: **10M+ global**, **2M+ US**, **72% satellite ISP market share**

## 3. Competitive Dynamics

### Wireless: T-Mobile Dominance Widening
- TMUS 35% → 40% by 2026; VZ 34% losing postpaid; T 27% growing modestly
- USCellular acquisition gives TMUS additional spectrum + rural coverage

### Broadband: Cable Under Siege from Three Fronts
1. **Fiber**: Record buildout (11.8M homes passed 2025); AT&T/VZ each target 40M+
2. **FWA**: T-Mobile alone 7.3M; Big 3 capacity for 32M total
3. **Satellite (Starlink)**: 10M+ global, 2M+ US, V3 satellites 2026 → gigabit speeds

### Tower REITs: DISH Fallout
- EchoStar spectrum sale/network shutdown → revenue risk for towers
- CCI most exposed ($3.5B+ claim); AMT also affected
- SBAC relatively insulated

### Convergence Trend
- CHTR/CMCSA ↔ TMUS business wireless MVNO
- TMUS entering fiber via Metronet
- T acquiring Lumen mass markets; VZ acquired Frontier
